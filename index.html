Personal project · active buildFRIDAY / SYSTEM 01

FRIDAY Media OS

A personal operating system for production control and evidence-driven media operations—built to make incomplete retrieval, ambiguous dates, and misleading status impossible to ignore.

FRIDAY exists because production work becomes difficult to trust when the system cannot prove what was retrieved, which day it belongs to, or whether a quiet screen means zero, failure, or incomplete data. I built it as a control plane that makes those states explicit for both operators and non-technical decision-makers.
