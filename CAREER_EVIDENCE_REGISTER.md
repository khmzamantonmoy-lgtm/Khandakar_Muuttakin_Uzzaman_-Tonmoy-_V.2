# Career Evidence Register

Status: Phase 1 working document  
Purpose: Track what can be published, what requires evidence, and what must remain private or unverified.

## Evidence status key

- **SUPPORTED** — Present in the current repository or explicitly supplied as a factual profile detail.
- **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** — The theme is present, but scope, dates, role, or outcome is incomplete.
- **NEEDS VERIFICATION** — Do not publish as fact until the user supplies evidence.
- **CONFIDENTIAL** — May be used only in anonymized form after approval.
- **PLANNED** — Future content or functionality, not a completed capability.
