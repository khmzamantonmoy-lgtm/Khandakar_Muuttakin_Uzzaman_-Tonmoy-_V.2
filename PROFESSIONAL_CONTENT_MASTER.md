## Publishing rules

- Use “worked with,” “supported,” “evaluated,” or “coordinated” only when the personal role is confirmed.
- Use “built,” “implemented,” or “designed” only when a repository, artifact, diagram, or other evidence exists.
- Use “achieved,” “reduced,” “improved,” or “delivered” only with a verifiable outcome.
- Use “in development,” “prototype,” or “planned” for incomplete FRIDAY functionality.
- Never publish the user’s competitive or reputational claims as factual evidence.
