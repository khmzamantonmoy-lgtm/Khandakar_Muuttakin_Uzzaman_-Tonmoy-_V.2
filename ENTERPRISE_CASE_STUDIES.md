# Enterprise Case Studies

Status: Phase 1 working document  
Confidentiality rule: Use sector-level anonymization unless the client, architecture, vendors, and engagement details are explicitly approved for publication.

## Case-study format

Every case study must answer:

1. What was the business context?
2. What problem or risk needed attention?
3. What was Tonmoy's verified role?
4. What solution approach was used?
5. Which technologies, products, or documentation were involved?
6. What outcome is evidenced?
7. What business decision became clearer?
8. What information must remain confidential?

Do not publish a customer name, architecture detail, vendor relationship, contract term, security control, financial value, or measurable outcome without permission.
